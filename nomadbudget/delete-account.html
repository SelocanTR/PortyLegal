Delete your Nomad Budget account

Nomad Budget, by Rubeeks (rubeeks.co)

In the app

  1. Open Nomad Budget and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

The account and everything in it are deleted at once. This cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, email nomadbudget@rubeeks.co from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my account. If you signed in with Apple and hid your address, write from the relay address Apple shows you. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

A guest account has no email address, so we cannot tell which one is yours. Delete it in the app by signing out of it before you uninstall.

What is deleted

Your account record, wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring rules, visits, crossings and marked countries, your profile and push token, and your subscription record with us. We also have RevenueCat, our subscription provider, delete its customer record for you. Wallets you shared disappear for their members. Entries you made in someone else's wallet stay there as their records, no longer attributed to you.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in system backups: purged within 30 days.
  • Usage data and crash reports: kept up to 90 days in all, no longer linked to any account.
  • Messages you sent with "Send feedback" or "Send this message to the developer": kept up to two years, no longer linked to any account. You can ask us to delete them sooner at the address above.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of a purchase under their own policies.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings, or it keeps renewing.
