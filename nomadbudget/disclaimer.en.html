Financial Disclaimer

Version 1.3 · Effective: 2026-09-07

Nomad Budget is a record-keeping and analysis tool. Every number it produces is an estimate derived from the data you entered and from open statistical sources.

1. Not Investment or Financial Advice

Nothing in the App — no summary, chart, comparison or suggestion — constitutes investment advice, financial advice, portfolio management or an investment recommendation.

Investment advice is provided by licensed institutions taking each person's risk and return preferences into account. The App holds no such licence and makes no personalised assessment.

2. Exchange Rates

Rates come from open, free exchange-rate services, are cached, and refresh periodically. They are not real-time, not binding for trading, and not the rate your bank will apply.

While offline, or when the rate service is unreachable, the last known rate is used. Converted amounts may therefore differ from what actually happens on your account.

For transactions dated in the past, the nearest available historical rate is used where one exists.

3. Cross-Country Price and Rent Comparisons

Price level comparisons are based on indices derived from World Bank ICP 2021 and Eurostat data, rolled forward with inflation and exchange rates. They are a rough estimate of country averages and do not reflect differences between cities, neighbourhoods or lifestyles.

Rent is the one item no free official source publishes on a comparable cross-country basis. The rent figures in the App are assembled for a small number of countries from sources that measure it directly, and those sources differ in nature (registered tenancy amounts, asking prices, or derived values). Where no data exists, the App asks you for the figure.

These comparisons are not sufficient on their own for a decision to relocate and are no substitute for local research.

4. Not Tax or Legal Advice

The App performs no tax calculation and gives no tax, accounting or legal advice. Your records are not official books, invoices or tax returns. Consult a qualified accountant regarding your tax obligations.

5. Data Accuracy and Backups

Every figure in the App rests on the data you entered; missing or mistaken entries affect every summary and chart.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no absolute guarantee against data loss can be given. Keep a separate copy of records that are critical to you.

6. Your Decisions

The consequences of any spending, saving, relocation or investment decision you make on the basis of information in the App are yours alone. No liability is accepted for losses arising from such decisions.
